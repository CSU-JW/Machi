# 黄色地标插画

使用内置 image_gen 生成，未使用 CLI。文件：train.png、radio.png、mallC.png、park.png。
黑灰未建成状态由界面 CSS 实现，原始彩色图片不修改。

共同提示词：
Use case: stylized-concept. Game landmark card illustration, square full bleed, no text numbers frame or watermark. Match a cozy storybook town board-game illustration, softly rendered detailed 3D cartoon architecture, warm stone, flowers, blue sky, sunny soft shadows. Dominant yellow and honey gold landmark palette. One clearly recognizable landmark centered, entire building visible, readable at thumbnail size.

各图 Subject：
- train: a charming small town railway station with golden clock tower and a little train
- radio: a charming town broadcasting center with tall radio antenna and golden signal dishes
- mallC: a charming grand shopping center with golden awnings and shop windows
- park: a charming amusement park with golden ferris wheel and carousel
